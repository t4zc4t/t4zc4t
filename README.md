I turn open questions into reproducible studies, and studies into platforms that keep themselves current. Every project starts from official data, publishes its method and sources alongside the code, and ends as something people can open and use.

Currently building **Jocasta**, an intelligence workstation where every record traces back to the evidence behind it.
